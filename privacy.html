Privacy Policy

Hot Mess Express · Effective October 26, 2026

In short: Hot Mess Express does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The only thing the game saves is your best score, and it stays on your device.

This policy explains how the Hot Mess Express game (the "app"), published by Deals4U ("we", "us"), treats your information. It applies to the Android app and to the web version of the game.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive information. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or any other personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves one piece of game data, your best score, in the app's private storage on your device so it is still there next time you play. This data:

  • never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else;
  • contains no personal information;
  • is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Hot Mess Express → Storage → Clear storage).

Internet access and permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. The Android framework the game is built with includes the standard internet permission, but the game itself does not send or receive any data: all of its graphics, sounds and fonts are built into the app, and it works fully offline.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party software that collects data. We do not share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Google Play, which you use to download and update the app, is operated by Google and may collect information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google on your device, Android may send Google anonymous crash and performance reports about apps, including this one; that is controlled by your device settings, not by the app.

Web version

If you play the game in a web browser, the website is served by a hosting provider that may automatically keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and operation. We do not use these logs to identify you, and the game itself stores your best score only in your own browser.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because no personal information is collected, none is ever collected from children.

Data security and retention

Since we do not collect or hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to store, secure or retain. Your best score stays on your device until you delete it as described above.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or delete. You can remove all game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding online features), we will update this policy and its effective date before those changes take effect.
